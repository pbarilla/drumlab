Your sounds and patterns, on a Pocket Operator, as audio.

1Plug in

Plug a cable from the FM-1's output into the PO-32's input. Turn MASTER up.
Get the PO-32 listening: acc + sound.

2Send it

Tap HOME until you're in the SEND view.
Hit PLAY. It asks first.
Hit OCT+. All 16 sounds, all 16 patterns, the tempo and the swing go over.
PLAY stops it.

3Play it there

Press play on the PO-32. That's your beat.
